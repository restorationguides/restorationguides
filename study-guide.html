Water Restoration Standards

We define water damage restoration as a controlled science centered on the ANSI/IICRC S500 standards.

Categories of Water

  • Category 1: Clean source (e.g., supply lines).
  • Category 2: Significant contamination (e.g., dishwasher).
  • Category 3: Grossly contaminated (e.g., sewage).

Classes of Intrusion

  • Class 1: < 5% of area affected.
  • Class 2: 5% - 40% of area affected.
  • Class 3: > 40% of area affected.
  • Class 4: Specialty drying (hardwood/concrete).

Technical Formulas

Dehumidifier: Cubic Feet / Class Factor = AHAM PPD Required
Air Filtration: (Cubic Feet * ACH) / 60 = CFM Required

Surface Maintenance & Fire Technicals

Advanced protocols derived from S100 and S700 standards for textile and soot mitigation.

S100: Textiles & Flooring

  • pH Logic: Alkaline cleaners for deep cleaning; Neutral pH for hardwood to prevent finish damage.
  • HWE: Hot Water Extraction (HWE) is the standard. Pure steam can damage upholstery/carpet fibers.
  • Crowning Prevention: Hardwood floors must be dried manually and immediately to avoid structural warping.
  • Acidic Solutions: Used to dissolve mineral buildup and surfactant residues.

S700: Smoke & Soot Profiles

  • Dry Soot (Natural): Wood/Cotton source. Use dry sponges or HEPA vacuums. Avoid wet cleaning initially.
  • Wet Soot (Synthetic): Plastic/Nylon source. Oily/Smeary. Requires alkaline degreasers and HWE.
  • Protein Soot: Kitchen fires. Invisible but pungent. Requires alkaline-enzyme cleaners.
  • Fuel Oil: Furnace puff-backs. Replace flex/fiberboard ducting; Clean metal ducts only after camera inspection.

Biohazard & Trauma Response

S540 Protocols

  • Establish legal medical waste manifesting.
  • Maintain Level C PPE at minimum for active remediation.
  • Sanitization of non-porous surfaces with broad-spectrum disinfectants.

Validation

  • Physical removal of all porous saturated materials.
  • Post-remediation ATP testing to verify sanitization.
