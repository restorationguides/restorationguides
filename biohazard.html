Biohazard & Trauma Response

Pathogen neutralization, medical waste compliance, and trauma scene remediation for we professionals.

Universal Precautions

We treat all biological fluids as if they are known to be infectious. Adherence to OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030 is non-negotiable for field safety.

PPE Level C

Baseline protection includes chemical-resistant suits, double-gloving, and full-face respirators with P100/Organic Vapor filtration.

Chain of Custody

Medical waste requires licensed transport and manifesting. We maintain strict records to mitigate long-term legal liability.

Remediation Strategy

Physical Removal

Saturated porous materials (carpet, cushion, drywall) are removed and discarded. We do not attempt to clean biological fluid from porous substrates.

ATP Validation Testing

We utilize bioluminescence monitoring to quantify biological soil levels and verify sanitization efficacy.

Decontamination Chambers

3-stage decon zones prevent cross-contamination. Buffer zones are utilized for transition and PPE donning/doffing.

Neutralization Logic

  • Dwell Time: We observe strict 10-minute wet-contact times for EPA-registered broad-spectrum disinfectants.
  • Surface Loading: Gross filth must be physically removed before the final application of disinfectants to ensure chemical efficacy.
  • Validation: Surfaces must be returned to a "Condition 1" (Sanitary) state prior to release.

Technical Brief: ATP Validation

We use Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP) monitoring to provide quantitative proof of cleanliness. When ATP reacts with luciferase in our swabs, it produces light measured in Relative Light Units (RLU).

Surface Result RLU Range Professional Action
Pass (Sanitary) 0 - 10 Documentation & Release
Caution 11 - 30 Re-clean & Re-test
Fail (Pathogen Risk) 31+ Immediate Re-remediation
Field Logic: ATP testing detects biological presence, indicating the surface can no longer support pathogen growth.
