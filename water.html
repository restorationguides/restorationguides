Advanced structural drying protocols, psychrometric management, and moisture extraction for we professionals.

Water Categories (Sanitary Description)

Category 1: Clean Water

Originates from a sanitary source (e.g., broken supply lines, tub overflow without contaminants).

Category 2: Gray Water

Significant contamination; potential to cause discomfort or illness (e.g., dishwasher/washing machine discharge).

Category 3: Black Water

Grossly contaminated; contains pathogenic, toxigenic, or other harmful agents (e.g., sewage, rising water).

Classes of Water (Evaporative Load)

  • Class 1: Least amount of water/evaporation (low porosity materials).
  • Class 2: Significant amount (carpet, cushion, 5-40% of room surfaces).
  • Class 3: Greatest amount (water from above, >40% of surfaces affected).
  • Class 4: Specialty drying (bound water in hardwood, brick, concrete).

Psychrometric Targets

Metric Target Range Professional Technical Logic
Relative Humidity Below 40% Encourages evaporation by maintaining low vapor pressure in the air.
Temperature 70°F - 90°F Increases the energy of water molecules and the air's moisture capacity.
GPP Depression > 5 GPP Verifies the dehumidifier is actively pulling moisture from the environment.
