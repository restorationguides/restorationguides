Our Roots

Made by Dylan Worth for the love of the field.

The Restoration Hub didn't start as a commercial project. It began as a personal guide—a way to help myself and a few colleagues wrap our heads around the complexities of the WRT certification. What started as a study sheet evolved into a comprehensive technical resource for we professionals who take pride in the science of restoration.

Our Mission

High-level technical information should be accessible, accurate, and field-ready. We bridge the gap between classroom theory and the variables we face on-site every day.

The Four Pillars

  • Precision: Data-driven drying.
  • Safety: Strict PPE protocols.
  • Integrity: Unbiased verification.
  • Mastery: Continuous education.

The Restoration Perspective

In this industry, we don't just "clean" surfaces—we restore environments. Whether it's Sunday at 7 AM or a midnight emergency call, we nerd out on the physics and chemistry of the job because that’s what it takes to do it right.
