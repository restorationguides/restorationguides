Mold Remediation

Containment engineering, microbial pressure management, and physical removal protocols for we professionals.

Source Containment

Utilized for minor, localized growth (< 10 sq ft). We use 6-mil poly to cover the immediate area and prevent spore release during removal.

Local (Structure) Containment

Used for moderate contamination. Seals off a specific room or area. Includes a single-flap entry and dedicated HEPA air filtration.

Full Containment

Required for extensive growth. Features multi-chamber decontamination zones (Clean, Shower, Dirty) and critical pressure monitoring.

Remediation Methodologies

HEPA Sanding & Wire Brushing

For semi-porous materials like wood studs. We utilize mechanical agitation combined with integrated HEPA vacuuming to trap spores at the source.

Pressure Differentials

We maintain a minimum of -0.02 inches of water gauge (in. W.G.) relative to the exterior to ensure zero-leakage of contaminants.

Post-Remediation Verification (PRV)

A mandatory independent assessment including visual inspection and air/surface sampling before containment is struck.

The 6 Limitations

We must account for these factors when applying antimicrobials:

  • Concentration: Correct dilution is vital for efficacy.
  • Temperature: Most chemicals fail in extreme cold/heat.
  • pH: Surface alkalinity can deactivate cleaners.
  • Organic Matter: Soil load can shield microbial spores.
  • Contact/Dwell Time: Essential for total "kill" rates.
